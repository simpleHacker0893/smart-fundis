# Starter prompts for the Claude.ai Project

- **Status check:** "Here are the latest handoff and review files. Where are we against the MVP loop, what's at risk, and what should I cut if I have 3 hours left?"
- **Swahili copy:** "Review `sw.json` against `en.json`. Flag anything that reads as a literal translation or sounds formal or harsh to a Nairobi fundi, and suggest better wording."
- **Rubric:** "Draft a 5-item rubric for <trade> / <task>. Every item must be visible on a 10–90 s phone video. Mark the safety items."
- **Feedback tone:** "Here's a Verdict JSON. Is `feedback_sw` as clear and kind as `feedback_en`? Rewrite both if needed."
- **RAI check:** "Walk through PRD §8 against this review report. What would a judge poke at?"
- **Pitch:** "Write a 90-second demo script and a 3-minute pitch from the demo-script and project-card files. Lead with the fundi's story."
- **Decision:** "Two options: <A> vs <B>. Recommend one for the demo and give the reason."
