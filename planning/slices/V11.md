# V11 — M-Pesa and Fundi Pro (sandbox)

- **Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-26-v2-payments-design.md` (Part B: §2, §4–§9, §12, §13) and `…-v2-marketplace-design.md` (§8.2, §8.4a, §10, US-8.13 WhatsApp part, US-8.31, US-8.32, §17 #15, #15a, #15b, #37–#39) · **Spec issue:** not published yet
- **ADRs:** ADR-27 (M-Pesa for Smart Fundis' own fees only; no escrow), ADR-28 (paid subscription and a labelled ranking boost; amends ADR-22 and D-24), ADR-9 (no inbound port on Brev; not affected) · **Decisions:** D-41, D-42, D-44, D-46, D-47, D-48, R2-2, R3-1, R4-1, R4-2, R6-6 (KSh 200 a month)
- **Lane:** mpesa (`convex/payments/`, contracts, no UI) + convex (schema wiring, `syncListing`, `contact.reveal`) + frontend (every screen, from mpesa's UI suggestions) + designer; rai-reviewer and qa at the end.
- **Blocked by:** V8 (`tierFor` V8-6, `notify` V8-8, `contact.reveal` V8-21, `interests.listForJob` V8-20); the operator listing the **mpesa** agent and its skills in `AGENTS.md` (Spec A §23, review §5) before V11-1. V9 comes first in order but is not a hard block.
- **Sandbox only.** Every payment surface shows **"Test mode — no real money"**. On the production deployment in sandbox, only Admins and `PAYMENTS_TESTERS` see a pay sheet, and their `mode: "sandbox"` subscriptions **never** set `listings.subscriber` (R-38). Going live needs V2-Q35 (Till and Org-portal access), V2-Q29 (tax), V2-Q30 (counsel) and the payments DPIA update; that is a human go-live checklist, not a ticket here.
- **"Fundi Pro" stays on `/roadmap`** until `DARAJA_ENV=production` on prod (§13 rule 4): real Fundis on prod can't buy it before then.
- **Package manager:** pnpm (D-12). Daraja keys in the Convex env and the root `.env` only, never `NEXT_PUBLIC_` (D-13).

## Demo at the end
On the dev deployment, at 360 px:
1. A Fundi opens **Get Fundi Pro**: the pay sheet shows KSh 200, "30 days", what it unlocks ("Clients can open your WhatsApp in one tap", "You appear above fundis without Fundi Pro … tagged 'FUNDI PRO · PAID'", and on Electrical and Solar "verified fundis still come first"), what it doesn't ("It never gives you a badge…"), the refund line and the **Test mode** banner.
2. They tap Pay; the sandbox STK prompt goes out; the screen reads "Check your phone and enter your M-Pesa PIN", then **"You're Fundi Pro until <date>"**. A second tap during the wait sends no second prompt.
3. On **`/fundis?trade=plumbing`** they now appear first with the **FUNDI PRO · PAID** tag button and the legend; tapping the tag opens the explanation. On **`/fundis?trade=electrical`** a verified Fundi still comes first.
4. A Client sees **Chat on WhatsApp** on the Pro Fundi's response and `/f/[id]`, with "Starting a WhatsApp chat shares your WhatsApp number with this fundi."
5. With fake timers: the 10-day renewal notice with **Renew**, then the lapse notice; the tag, the first place and the WhatsApp button disappear.
6. An Admin at **`/admin/payments`** runs "Check with M-Pesa now" and records a refund with an Org-portal reference.

## Grill focus
1. The V2-Q49 and V2-Q50 answers, and what changes if STK Query can't confirm an ID we never stored (R-40).
2. The result codes marked UNVERIFIED in Spec B §5.1, and the fallback copy for unknown codes.
3. The test-mode gate on prod: `PAYMENTS_TESTERS` matched on the token's verified email, and proof that a tester's sandbox subscription never reorders real results (R-38).
4. Paid ranking and consumer protection (R-37): is the tag + legend + "Verified only" enough, and does the SW legend read right to a native speaker?
5. The refund rule when a refund ends the period mid-Job (E21): what the Client's open response list shows.
6. Who owns which file: mpesa writes `convex/payments/**`; convex spreads `paymentTables` into `schema.ts` and calls `registerPaymentRoutes(http)` in `http.ts`; frontend alone writes `web/`.
