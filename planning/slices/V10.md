## Demo at the end
At 360 px:
1. A Client on **Post a job** types "fix my probox brakes ruiru around 3k". The keyword chip **Mechanic** appears at once, with no AI.
2. They tap **Suggest from my words**. Within 15 s the fields fill in as editable values marked **"AI suggestion — check before posting"**: Trade Mechanic, title "Fix Probox brakes", county Kiambu, ward Ruiru matched from the picker, budget KSh 3,000.
3. They change the title and publish. Nothing was published before the tap on Publish.
4. With the Brev box off (a stale heartbeat), the same screen shows only the keyword chip: **no AI box, no spinner, no error**.
5. An Admin sees the **"Trade kept as suggested"** tile split by language (en / sw / mixed).

## Grill focus
1. The go / no-go numbers: what p95 is acceptable while Cosmos is busy, and what running Brev around the clock costs.
2. Whether GPU memory on the one box holds Cosmos 8B and Nemotron Nano together, or the parse lane competes with video (gpu-devops).
3. Kiswahili and Sheng quality (R-20, R-29): the sample set and who checks it before the box is shown in SW.
4. The strip-before-queue filter: is anything personal left in a one-sentence job description after phones, emails and URLs are removed?
5. The "Trade kept" metric: does it measure anything useful when the keyword chip already suggested the same Trade?
