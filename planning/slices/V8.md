# V8 — Clients and Jobs

- **Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-26-v2-marketplace-design.md` (§2–§9, §12–§17) and `…-v2-payments-design.md` Part A (direct pay) · **Spec issue:** not published yet
- **ADRs:** ADR-21 (opt-in contact reveal, kept for Call), ADR-22 (Listing projection), ADR-23 (Client is a derived role), ADR-24 (Jobs, Interests, contact only through logged steps), ADR-26 (price is a filter, never a rank), ADR-27 (no escrow) · **Decisions:** D-30, D-31, D-33 to D-35, D-38, D-40, D-41, D-43, D-45 to D-47 (no Pro copy yet), D-49, D-53, D-56, D-57
- **Lane:** A (convex + frontend + auth) + designer; rai-reviewer and qa at the end.
- **Two phases** (like V6):
  - **Core, "post and browse":** V8-0a to V8-16. A Client posts a Job and it shows on `/jobs`.
  - **Rest, "respond and hire":** V8-0b, V8-17 to V8-26. A Fundi responds and the Client hires.
  Both phases deploy to dev only. **Production waits for the rest phase** and for the gates below, so the JOBS tab never goes live on a board nobody can answer (§13 rule 4).
- **Blocked by:** V7 (roles, shell); the **V6 core** (V6-1 `listings`, V6-2 `/fundis`) for everything (D-45); **V2-Q11 names part** (the IEBC ward list, D-56) for V8-2. The rest phase is blocked by the core, and V8-21 by V6-4 and V6-5.
- **Production gates (D-49, §12.4):** the DPIA update for Client accounts and Jobs, processor agreements on file for Convex, Clerk, Brev and LangSmith (with their processing regions confirmed), the 72-hour breach runbook, and the native-speaker sign-off on every consent string (R-20, V8-25) **before merge**.
- **No Pro wording anywhere** (rai S2): `listings.subscriber` is false for everyone, the response list reads "Verified first", and contact is the Call reveal only.
- **Package manager:** pnpm (D-12). `@convex-dev/rate-limiter` is already mounted (#29).

## Demo at the end of the core
At 360 px:
1. A visitor taps **Post a job**, signs in and completes **`/client/start`**: display name, phone ("Fundis never see your number"), county and ward, "I am 18 or older", and the Client terms in EN + SW with the processing-outside-Kenya line.
2. They post "Fix Probox brakes": the keyword map suggests **Mechanic**, they pick Kiambu → Ruiru → Gitothua from the IEBC picker, add a landmark and a budget ("Budget set by the client"), and add a photo with the per-photo tick. The Publish screen restates what goes public.
3. The Job appears on **`/jobs`** (header **JOBS** tab) and in the landing page's **Latest jobs** strip, showing Trade, title, county · ward, budget, age and "No responses yet". No description, photo or landmark in the public HTML.
4. Demo Jobs sort after it, tagged "DEMO: NOT A REAL JOB".
5. The Client sees the Job in their dashboard. A signed-in Fundi reports it; an Admin removes it at **`/admin/jobs`**, and the Client sees "Removed by Smart Fundis: <reason>. Questions: info@smartfundis.com" and a notice under the bell.

## Demo at the end of V8
1. A Listed Fundi with no Pay-to details opens the Job: "I'm interested" opens the Pay-to form first (with the Pochi warning), then the note. The scam line shows on the Job.
2. The Client's dashboard shows "1 new response". The response list reads **"Verified first"**; the Client shortlists, taps **Call** and gets a `tel:` link (view-source before the tap has no number), then **Hire**, and sees the Pay-to details with the name-check line.
3. The Fundi sees "Hired" in **My responses**, the landmark and the Client's full display name, and never the Client's number.
4. A signed-in User without a Client profile taps Call on `/f/[id]` and lands on `/client/start`. `/f/[id]` has no WhatsApp button.
5. With fake timers, the hired Job auto-closes after 30 days and the Client gets the notice.
