## Demo at the end
At 360 px:
1. A Fundi signs in and opens **`/dashboard`**, which lands on the Fundi dashboard (the only role they hold). The role bar is a bottom nav; the EN | SW toggle is in the menu sheet.
2. The dashboard shows their Assessment status chips, profile completeness and Listing status ("Listed · Not yet verified").
3. They tap **Add video**: the sheet offers **Record / Upload** ("Earns a badge once an Expert approves it") and **YouTube / TikTok link** ("Showcase — not verified"). Record opens the existing Assessment flow; a pasted YouTube link is saved as a Showcase link.
4. Two stub Assessments, one AI `pass` and one capped `needs_review`, both show exactly **"Awaiting expert review"**.
5. A seeded Expert (who is also a Fundi) switches role in the avatar sheet, sees the queue with a "Safety check" marker on one row and no AI chip, opens it, taps a **0:42** chip (plain text until the timestamp eval passes), plays the video to the end, and only then sees "AI suggestion — you decide". They choose reshoot with a note.
6. The Fundi sees the note, the AI feedback and "The AI noticed…" (English evidence labelled "AI note in English", no timestamps until the eval passes, no jump) on the reshoot, and **Record again**. On an approved one, the Badge line, the feedback and the same "The AI noticed…" list show, framed as practice notes (D-59, D-61).
7. An Admin opens **`/admin/ops`** and sees the tiles, including "Expert decisions, not accuracy" with one agreement row.

## Grill focus
1. The exact byte-identical rule (D-50): which fields may differ between a `pass` and a capped row (only ids and times), and how the chip glyph avoids hinting.
2. The play-through signal: `ended` fires once, but what about seeking to the end, a playback-rate change, or a video that fails to load? Is a seek to the end "played through"?
3. Whether the timestamp chips stay plain text for all of V7 (the eval that checks reliability is an MVP V5 artefact).
4. Profile completeness: which fields count, and making sure it never reads as a score or a verification.
5. The Add video sheet: how the Showcase link is validated without fetching it, and the copy that stops a Fundi thinking a TikTok link earns a Badge.
6. Where the counters are bumped for events that happen in HTTP actions (callbacks) versus crons, so that none is double-counted on a retry.
