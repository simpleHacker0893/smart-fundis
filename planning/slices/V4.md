## Demo at the end
1. **Expert application:** a user applies to be an Expert, sees `/application-pending`, and an Admin approves them for Hairdressing. They now see `/expert` in the avatar menu.
2. **Appeal:** a Fundi appeals a rejection, giving a reason. A **different** Expert decides it, or an Admin if there's no other Expert.
3. **Override:** an Admin overrides a decision with a reason, and the Badge appears or disappears.
4. **Deletion:** a Fundi deletes a video once review is finished, and the Badge stays.
5. **Failure:** a `failed` Assessment shows "Record again".
6. **Tests:** the role-bypass tests pass.

## In scope
- `expertApplications` submit, list, approve and reject; `/application-pending`; the Admin screens (plain shadcn).
- Appeal, including the routing when there's no other Expert.
- The override mutation, with the UI coming last in the cut order.
- Video deletion, with its guards.
- The "Record again" link that sets `previousAssessmentId`.
- The US-2.8 `convex-test` role-bypass suite.

## Grill focus
1. Whether an Admin can approve their own Expert application.
2. What happens to an Expert's open reviews when an Admin deactivates them.
3. Whether an override *to* `approved` of a demo Assessment is allowed. Probably not.
4. The wording of the audit log for each action.
