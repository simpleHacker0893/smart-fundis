## Demo at the end
At 360 px:
1. A Fundi opens **Where I work**, reads the location consent line, taps **Use my location** (with "Pick your usual work spot, not your home"), sees **"Near Gitothua"**, and picks a 10 km radius.
2. Their Fundi home shows **"Jobs near you"**: the first three with bands ("2–5 km"). The Jobs tab lists up to 60 Jobs sorted by band, then newest, with a **Refresh** button.
3. A visitor on **`/jobs`** taps **Near me**: the Job cards show bands computed from each Job's ward centre. With permission denied, the control reads **"Location off — pick a county"** and the county filter stays.
4. On **`/fundis?trade=plumbing`** with near me, the Listing cards show bands, verified first.
5. On a Job's responses, each response shows its band, and **Within 10 km** filters them.
6. The Fundi taps **Turn off location**: they drop out of near-me results at once and still appear under their county. A Job in Marsabit shows "In Marsabit" and "Distance isn't available in Marsabit yet".

## Grill focus
1. The V9-2 answer: if `nearest()` can't combine `maxDistance` with filter keys cheaply, is the ring search good enough for the six counties?
2. The centroid source licence (HDX vs OSM) and the attribution line in the footer.
3. The triangulation floor (R-41): is the "about 1 km" copy accurate for a Fundi who sets their device point, and is 10 moves a day the right `serviceAreaSet` figure?
4. How `setJobStatus` and `syncListing` keep the index in step when a Job closes or a Fundi is hidden, and what a crash between the table write and the component write leaves behind (both run in one mutation?).
5. The bandwidth figure at 10k Listings and 2k open Jobs: measured, not claimed (§7.3).
