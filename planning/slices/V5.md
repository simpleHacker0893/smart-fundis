- **Spec issue:** not published yet

## Demo at the end
- **The seed:** `/fundis` shows about 20 Demo profiles, each Badge tagged "Demo: not a real verification", alongside one real Badge created live.
- **The eval:** `eval/summary.md` reports agreement %, safety-fault recall and p50/p95 latency for 8B, and 2B if time allows, broken down by Trade, gender and lighting.
- **Submission:** a backup demo video recorded before the freeze, `docs/demo-script.md` (90 seconds), and `docs/project-card.md`.

## In scope
- `seed.demo`: idempotent, sets `isDemo`, invented names, initials avatars, no videos, and demo Assessments never in the queue. Run it with `seed --prod`.
- `POST /eval/run` over `eval/clips.csv`, which writes `results.csv` and `summary.md`. The labels are only compared after the run.
- The project card covers:
  - GPU and hours
  - the models and prompt versions
  - what ran on Brev and why (ADR-9)
  - the eval figures
  - data sources (own recordings, and Pexels with its licence)
  - **everything prepared in advance**: the PRD, specs, ADRs, prompts, the Stitch prompts, eval labels, `.claude/` and `planning/`
  - the known limitations
