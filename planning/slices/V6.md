## Demo at the end of the core (before V5)
At 360 px, a Client with no account:
1. Opens **`/trades`** and sees every Trade with a photo, "Find a fundi →" on each, and the legend (✓ Verified · Expert verifier · Not yet verified).
2. Taps **Electrical** and lands on **`/fundis?trade=electrical`**.
3. Sees verified cards first (Badge lines, one "Expert verifier · Electrical"), then Demo cards, then "Not yet verified" cards.
4. Toggles **Verified only**, then sets county **Kiambu** and area **Ruiru**.
5. Taps a card and gets the V1 unstyled `/f/[id]`.

## Demo at the end of V6
1. The profile shows Badges, the Expert mark, **Recent work** (each item "Not verified"), **Rates** ("Set by the fundi — not verified"), Showcase and opted-in links, and **Call / WhatsApp**. Tapping Call reveals the number; view-source before the tap has no number.
2. In **"My public profile"**, the Fundi:
   - uploads a photo, publishes it through the bilingual Portfolio consent, and picks it as the cover
   - turns on "Show my phone to clients"
   - turns on the CV link and sees the home-address warning
   - adds two rate lines
3. A Client reports the photo. An Admin hides it at `/admin/reports`, and the Fundi sees "Hidden by Smart Fundis: <reason>. Questions: info@smartfundis.com".

## Grill focus
