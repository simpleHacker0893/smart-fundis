## Demo at the end
1. At 360 px, a Client with no account opens **`/trades`**, taps **Electrical → Find a fundi**, lands on **`/fundis?trade=electrical`**, sets county **Kiambu** and area **Ruiru**, and sees a grid of cards (cover photo or initials, name, county · area, Badge lines, Demo tag where seeded).
2. They tap a card: **`/f/[id]`** shows Badges ("Verified by Smart Fundis — …"), **Recent work** (photos and a tap-to-play video, each "Not verified"), **Rates** ("Set by the fundi — not verified"), Showcase links (tap to load), and **Call / WhatsApp**. Tapping Call reveals the number as a `tel:` link. View-source before the tap has no number.
3. The Fundi opens **"My public profile"**: uploads a photo, sees it private, publishes it through the Portfolio consent (English and Kiswahili), picks it as cover, turns on "Show my phone to clients", adds two rate lines, and watches the preview update live.
4. The Client taps **Report** on the photo. An Admin sees it at `/admin/reports`, hides the photo with a reason, and it disappears from the profile; the Fundi sees "Hidden by Smart Fundis: <reason>".
