# paper-code-tip — upload tips and onboarding (round 2)

- **Ratio:** `PORTRAIT_4_5`
- **Focal point:** the paper with the code, in the centre.

## Prompt
Photorealistic close-up of a person's hands holding a torn piece of white paper toward a phone camera. The paper shows three large handwritten digits, "315", in black marker. Behind it, slightly out of focus, a half-braided cornrow on a salon chair headrest and a wide-tooth comb. Warm indoor light. No faces, no other text, no logos. A soft deep green (#0B5D3B) background tone.

## Accept only if
- Exactly 3 legible digits appear, with no other text.
- There are no faces. A back of a head with hair is fine, but no facial features.
