## What changed
- `ai-service/app/nemotron.py`: `nemotron_smoke(settings)` builds `ChatNVIDIA(model, api_key, temperature=0)` and calls `.with_structured_output(SmokeReply{trade, ok, note})` with a fixed prompt that holds no user data, inside `tracing.pipeline_context` (masked, D-14). The CLI is `python -m app.nemotron`.
- **Secrets:**
  - `_scrub` redacts the key in its raw, URL-encoded and base64 forms, plus any `nvapi-…` and `lsv2_…` token, before truncating.
  - Errors are raised with an **empty exception chain**.
  - A `flush()` failure can't replace the result.
- `app/tracing.py`: `configure_tracing` copies only `LANGSMITH_*` from the root `.env` into `os.environ`. `NVIDIA_API_KEY` never lands there.
- `app/settings.py`: a blank or whitespace-only value counts as unset, in one place.
- `app/prompts/nemotron_smoke.v1.txt`, `tests/test_nemotron.py`, `tests/fixtures/nemotron_smoke_response.json`, README, and the `langchain-nvidia-ai-endpoints>=1.4.3` dependency.
- Docs: QUESTIONS #4 answered, **R-14** added, and **D-13** amended to reconcile story 26.
