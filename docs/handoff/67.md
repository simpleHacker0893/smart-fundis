## What changed
**Route groups and shell**
- `web/app/layout.tsx` no longer renders the site header.
  - `(site)` and `(auth)` wrap their pages in `components/site-chrome.tsx` (the header plus `#main-content`), so the marketing and auth pages look as they did.
  - The new `web/app/(app)/` group holds `dashboard`, `fundi` and `expert` (moved with `git mv`, same URLs). `onboarding` stays in `(site)`.
- `web/components/app-shell/` (new, split into small files). No marketing header or footer on role routes.
  - **Desktop (≥ 1024 px):** a 248 px categorized sidebar that collapses to a 64 px icon rail (the state is kept in localStorage, wrapped in try/catch).
  - **Mobile (< 1024 px):** a 56 px header, a bottom nav with up to 3 items plus **More**, and a More sheet that holds the same grouped menu.
  - An avatar sheet with initials, name, email, Manage account and Sign out.
  - A **DASHBOARD** role switch, shown only for users with both roles. It appears in the avatar sheet and the desktop sidebar footer, as buttons with `aria-pressed`.
  - An offline strip and a "Back online" strip.
- `web/lib/app-nav.ts`: the menus per role, per D2 and D-65.
  - **Fundi:** Add video, then OVERVIEW, VERIFICATION, PROFILE, SMART FUNDIS and ACCOUNT.
  - **Expert:** REVIEW, SMART FUNDIS and ACCOUNT.
  - No History, and no V8 items.

**Primitives**
- `components/ui/pill.ts`: an `app-primary` variant and `APP_PRIMARY_PILL` with no glow. The landing pill is unchanged.
- `components/ui/status-chip.tsx`: a neutral 28 px chip showing a glyph plus words (D4), with `data-testid="status-chip"` and `data-status`. It never uses amber or ✓.
- `components/ui/app-type.ts`: the D1 type ladder classes.
- `components/app-states.tsx`: the shared empty, error, skeleton and error-line states. The ✕ glyph is `aria-hidden`.
- `components/badge-line.tsx`: one Badge line, shared with `/f/[id]`.

**Fundi pages**
- `/fundi`, the home page:
  - the title "Get your work verified.";
  - the latest 3 verifications, with a status announcer;
  - YOUR BADGES;
  - the Listing status card, hidden when there's no profile;
  - "Still to add: First video".
- `components/add-video-sheet.tsx`: the Add video sheet, rendered once at the shell root through `AddVideoProvider`. It's a bottom sheet on mobile and an anchored popover on desktop.
  - **Record or upload** leads to `/fundi/record`, and the sheet says it earns a Badge.
  - **YouTube or TikTok link** is saved as Showcase ("SHOWCASE — NOT VERIFIED", "Links never earn a Badge.").
  - The sheet checks the link's host, warns before replacing a saved link, and shows "Needs a connection" when offline.
- `/fundi/record`: the #38 upload flow, restyled.
  - The steps read 1 PICK A TASK, 2 YOUR CODE, 3 RECORD and 4 CONSENT AND UPLOAD.
  - The only progress bar is the real upload one, in white.
  - The Upload button is disabled when offline.
  - When the upload finishes, it links to "See my verifications".
- `/fundi/verifications` and `/fundi/verifications/[id]`: the list, and a detail page for every status.
  - `awaiting_review` shows the same line whatever the Verdict (D-50).
  - The Expert's note appears only on reshoot and rejected, never on approved (#41).
- `/fundi/showcase`: the Showcase links editor, restyled.

**Expert pages**
- `/expert`, the queue:
  - the title "Review queue";
  - counts by Trade and the oldest waiting age, worked out in the browser;
  - a Trade filter, with a filtered-empty state;
  - a neutral "SAFETY CHECK" marker;
  - no AI chip.
- `/expert/[assessmentId]`, the review:
  - two columns on desktop, with the player sticky on mobile;
  - the Liveness readout, then Observations with safety items first and plain-text timestamps;
  - the **AI panel stays locked until the video's first `ended` event**. This is on-page only: the server-side gate is #52;
  - the decision sits below the AI panel. The choices are Approve, Ask for a new video and Reject, with none pre-selected;
  - the note label for Approve reads "for the record — not shown to the Fundi";
  - a counter up to 1000, and a hint when the button is disabled;
  - Submit is disabled when offline;
  - a failed submit keeps the note and the choice.
- **Copy:** every string is in `web/messages/en.json` only (D-64). New or rewritten namespaces: `AppShell`, `StatusChip`, `FundiHome`, `AddVideo`, `Verifications`, `Showcase`, `RecordPage`, `ExpertPage`, `ReviewQueue`, `ReviewDetail` and `DecisionForm`.
- **e2e (qa):** `upload.spec.ts`, `full-loop.spec.ts` and `onboarding.spec.ts` follow the new flow: home, then `/fundi/record`, then the chip on `/fundi` through the bottom nav. `helpers.ts` gains `tapBottomNav`.
- **Docs:** D-65 is marked Architect-accepted in `planning/DECISIONS.md` and DESIGN.md D2.
