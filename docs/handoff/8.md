## The Cosmos client contract (V2 depends on this)
Measured on vLLM 0.30.0 with `--reasoning-parser qwen3`:
1. **Always append NVIDIA's reasoning suffix** (`cosmos_reason2_utils.text.REASONING_PROMPT`): "Answer the question using the following format: `<think>` Your reasoning. `</think>` Write your final answer immediately after the `</think>` tag."
   - Then `message.reasoning` holds the trace and `message.content` holds the answer.
   - Without the suffix the model emits no `</think>`, and the parser puts the whole answer in `reasoning` with `content: null` (`finish_reason: stop`). The coordinator reproduced this with a plain "Reply with the single word: ready" prompt.
   - NVIDIA's README serves with the parser on and runs its examples with `--reasoning`, so I kept the parser and chose the suffix, rather than dropping the parser.
   - The V2 client should read `content` and treat an empty `content` as a failed call.
2. **fps goes in `media_io_kwargs`:** `extra_body={"media_io_kwargs": {"video": {"fps": 4}}}`. It needs the server flag `--media-io-kwargs '{"video": {"num_frames": -1}}'`. Setting `mm_processor_kwargs.fps` alone is ignored: vLLM samples about 2 fps (16 frames for 8 s).
3. **Cap the video with `mm_processor_kwargs.size`:** `{"shortest_edge": 131072, "longest_edge": (max_model_len - max_tokens) * 1024 * 0.9}`, as in NVIDIA's `inference.py`. `longest_edge` is the total pixel budget across all frames.
4. **Never send `do_sample_frames: true`.** NVIDIA's client sends it, but on vLLM 0.30.0 it makes `Qwen3VLProcessor` fail with HTTP 400 (`Failed to apply Qwen3VLProcessor`).
5. **Sampling:** use NVIDIA's reasoning defaults, temperature 0.6 and top_p 0.95. NVIDIA recommends max_tokens of 4096 or more to avoid truncation. With max_model_len 8192, that halves the video budget; the smoke uses 1024 and checks `finish_reason`.

## The L4 caveat
- The ticket and the runbook assume an H100 80 GB. This box is an L4 with 24 GB. NVIDIA's 8B card says "minimum of 32 GB" and was tested on H100 and A100.
- 8B BF16 does serve here, but only with a small context (8192), a KV cache of 12,800 tokens (one or two requests at once), and 1–2 GB of headroom.
- Consequences:
  - Nemotron can't be self-hosted beside it (D-32 / G6 assume one H100).
  - Nemotron has to stay hosted on this box.
  - The 2B fallback swaps in for the 8B; it can't run alongside it.
  - The 90 s clip loses detail at 8192 (R-3).
- The box choice needs a recorded decision. The architect owns that; I didn't touch DECISIONS.md.
