## What changed
- `web/app/globals.css`: **the only theme source.**
  - Dark-only tokens in `:root`: `--bg #050609`, `--panel`, `--line`, `--text #f2f4f7`, `--dim`, `--faint`, `--amber #ef9a57`.
  - Every shadcn token maps onto those. **Amber is only `--primary` and `--ring`**, never `--accent`, so shadcn's hover surfaces stay neutral (the 95/5 rule).
  - Fonts through `@theme inline`, `tabular-nums`, an amber focus ring and a `readout` utility.
- `web/app/layout.tsx`:
  - Inter and JetBrains Mono through `next/font`, and `class="dark"`.
  - Page order: `SiteHeader`, then `#main-content`, then `SiteFooter`, all inside the Clerk, Convex and next-intl providers.
  - Clerk `appearance` from `lib/clerk-appearance.ts`.
  - `themeColor` repeats `#050609`, because a meta tag can't read a CSS variable. A test checks it equals `--bg`.
- `web/components/`: `site-header` (skip link, logo, 48 px mobile and 72 px desktop, "Verified skills" tag at `lg`), `header-account`, `mobile-menu` (accessible Sheet with no motion), `site-footer`, `site-logo`, `ui/pill.ts` (shared pill classes) and `use-signed-in`.
- `web/lib/site-nav.ts`: the full Stitch nav and footer lists, filtered by **`BUILT_ROUTES`**. A link appears only once its page exists.
- `web/messages/en.json`: the `Shell`, `Footer` and `Links` namespaces, with no duplicate labels.

## Decisions and deviations (recorded here)
- **Bottom tab bar omitted in V0,** even though HANDOFF §2 lists it. All four of its tabs (`/evidence`, `/trades`, `/telemetry`, `/about`) point at pages that don't exist yet, and the honesty rule wins. Add it when those pages ship.
- **The desktop nav and the menu's nav rows are empty** until their routes join `BUILT_ROUTES`. The COMPANY dropdown is deferred until `/about` and `/contact` exist.
- **A signed-in user briefly sees "Sign in / Join"** until Clerk loads, and then Dashboard. Reading the session on the server with `auth()` in the layout would make `/` dynamic, so the flash is accepted.
- **Design choices, kept after the design review:**
  - a `--line` border under the header, not the amber hairline (HANDOFF §6)
  - the logo is the DESIGN.md SVG
  - the footer group labels are dim, not amber (95/5)
  - the footer headline is uppercase font-black, as in the exports
  - an outlined Dashboard pill when signed in, since no design exists for it
