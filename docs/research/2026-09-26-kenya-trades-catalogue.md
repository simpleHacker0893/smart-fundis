# Kenyan fundi trades: signup picker research

Researched 2026-09-26. There are 62 trades: the 12 existing slugs, kept exactly as they are, plus 50 new ones.

**How to read this**

- **Category** is one of three values. `skilled` means a formal trade test or curriculum exists and the work takes real training. `semi_skilled` means the trade is learned on the job and uses some tools. `odd_job` means general labour or a household service.
- Every Kiswahili name and description is a **SW draft**. It uses everyday Kenyan wording, including loanwords such as "gypsum" and "tiles" where fundis actually use them. A native speaker still needs to check all of it (R-20).
- **Licence** records whether a Kenyan regulator requires a licence or registration beyond a county single business permit, which every business needs.
- **Source keys** are listed at the end. [N4] is the primary source: the government's gazetted list of trade-testable areas. A number such as N4#79 is the item number in that list.
- **UNVERIFIED** means I could not confirm the item from a live, trustworthy page during this research.

## Merged or left out, and why

- **Merged:** plastering is part of masonry, as it is in the NITA Mason trade. Dressmaking is part of tailoring. NITA (N4#13 and #19) and CDACC keep them as separate standards, so split them if the Expert rubrics need to. Terrazzo is part of tiling. Henna and waxing are part of beauty. Boda riding is part of driving. Wi-Fi setup is part of the TV dish trade. Electric fences are part of CCTV. Appliance repair is part of electronics. House help is covered by cleaning.
- **Left out on purpose:**
  - Doctors, lawyers, engineers, architects and other licensed professionals.
  - Tutoring: a teaching service regulated by the TSC, not a trade.
  - Security guards: every guard must be individually registered with PSRA under the Private Security Regulation Act 2016, and a firm may not employ an unregistered guard [PSRA]. It is not a small-job trade.
  - Nannies and caregivers: this raises safeguarding problems, and video can't show the skill. NITA does list "Health Care Assistant" (N4#95).
  - DJs and MCs: entertainment rather than a trade.
  - Graphic design: digital and professional work (N4#117).
  - LPG gas fitting: niche work that EPRA licenses (UNVERIFIED).
  - Plant operators and scaffolders: employed by contractors (N4#34, #36, #65).
